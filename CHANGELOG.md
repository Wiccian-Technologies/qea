# Changelog

Key milestones of QEA.

## 2.4 ✦ 25 Nov 2026 ✦ Build It In First

QEA moves to the start of the making: read it before creating, check while creating (also through an AI assistant's hook), on every change and before it goes out, with a full check now and then as the safety net. Copies of other pages follow QEA rather than one to one, and projects link QEA instead of copying it.

## 2.3 ✦ 18 Nov 2026 ✦ Lotus Flower of Life

The Lotus Flower of Life replaces the Flower of Life throughout QEA, for its higher frequency: twelve circles opening from one centre, with how to draw it. It stays open, never enclosed by a circle, and pages draw on many sacred symbols instead of repeating one.

## 2.2 ✦ 15 Nov 2026 ✦ Open Source

QEA is published as an open source framework. Anyone can read it, use it and build on it.

## 2.0 ✦ 15 Nov 2026 ✦ Nine Dimensions

## 1.5 ✦ 15 Nov 2026 ✦ The Whole Circle
