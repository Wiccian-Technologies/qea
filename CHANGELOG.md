- Nine true dimensions: **Sound & Music** is new (432 Hz tuning, the Solfeggio frequencies, brainwave ranges, tempo and lengths, the sounds of the elements and seasons, sound in products); **Products, Spaces & Experiences** is its own dimension (Feng Shui directions in a room, light, sessions, group sizes, programmes).

- Elements & Seasons: the five Feng Shui elements with their seasons, shapes and colours, the nourishing, controlling and draining cycles for colour balance, and an aligned palette for each season (`palette/seasons.*`).
- Symbols: sacred geometry forms and the signs of New Homeopathy (Erich Körbler).
- Numbers: Grabovoi sequences, used as written for intention.
- Practices, not promises: a note on using traditions without health or money claims.
- A worked example for a web product; a table of contents for GitBook.
