# 4 ✦ Elements

∞ ∞ ∞

We identify the elements that dominate a person, a brand or a product, and balance them with the ones that steady and the ones that soften. Air, for example, is movement, change and life force: not a physical element but the energy that runs through everything.

## The Order

Wherever content can be grouped by the elements, it follows this order:

| | Element | Qualities | Typical themes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🜃 | **Earth** | ground, body, stability, abundance | food, home and land; foundations, money, structure |
| 🜄 | **Water** | flow, feeling, healing, cleansing | health and healing; relationships, emotions |
| 🜂 | **Fire** | warmth, passion, creation, transformation | art and gatherings; action, celebration |
| 🜁 | **Air** | movement, mind, communication, life force | learning and services; ideas, exchange |
| ✧ | **Ether** | spirit, space, connection, the whole | spirit and community; purpose, the field |

Example (Herz): a place's categories are earth (food, home and land) · water (health and healing) · fire (art and gatherings) · air (learning and services) · ether (spirit and community), drawn with the element symbols; a dashboard groups its numbers the same way, from the exchanges (earth) to the gestures (ether).

## Balancing

1. **Read the dominant element:** what a brand already radiates (e.g. much Fire: urgency, red, bold claims).
2. **Steady it** with the element that holds it (Fire is held by Water: calm blues, space, slower motion).
3. **Soften it** with the element it flows into (Fire flows into Earth: grounded neutrals, clear structure).
4. Keep **Ether** present in every piece: some space, a ✦, a sense of the whole.

## In Design

- Earth: neutrals (Sand, Ivory, Deep Taupe), solid shapes, generous grounding margins.
- Water: blues and sages, soft curves, flowing layouts.
- Fire: warm accents (Terracotta, Coral, Gold), used sparingly for the one main action.
- Air: whitespace, light type, gentle motion (360–1440 ms).
- Ether: Leela and Lumina, sacred geometry, ✦ and ∞.
