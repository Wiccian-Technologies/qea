# 9 ✦ Products, Spaces & Experiences

∞ ∞ ∞

Everything a brand offers can carry the same code: the physical product in the hand, the space people walk into, the session or event they live through. The rules of the other dimensions apply here together; standards and safety requirements always win.

## Physical Products

- **Dimensions and quantities:** 9 × 9 cm cards, 36 or 108 pieces in a pack, 54 ml or 108 ml bottles, 3 or 9 items in a set.
- **Colours:** from the brand's aligned palette; a season's palette for a seasonal edition (see [Elements & Seasons](3-elements-seasons.md)).
- **Shapes:** chosen by element: circles and arches for Metal, squares for Earth, waves for Water, points for Fire, tall forms for Wood.
- **Symbols:** on packaging, inside a lid, embossed or printed quietly (see [Symbols](4-symbols.md)).
- **Intention:** one sentence in the documentation or on the inside of the packaging.
- **Standards win:** paper sizes (A4, A5), shipping and safety rules, legal labels and measurements.

## Digital Products

Apps, websites and tools carry every other dimension at once: aligned numbers and colours, element-ordered content, symbols, words, quiet sound and compressed media, built from tokens in code. See the [worked example](../examples/web-product.md).

## Spaces

Shops, studios, offices and event venues.

- **Feng Shui directions:** Wood in the east, Fire in the south, Earth in the centre, Metal in the west, Water in the north; place colours, materials and shapes where their element lives.
- **Balance:** a space with too much of one element feels it (a room of glass and steel is very Metal: add wood and plants; a dark, damp room is very Water: add warm light and earth tones).
- **Light:** warm light (2700–3000 K) for rest and welcome, neutral (3500–4000 K) for work; daylight first.
- **Sound:** music tuned to 432 Hz at a calm volume, or the sounds of the season (see [Sound & Music](6-sound-music.md)).
- **Measurements:** aligned where they are free (shelf heights, table sizes, the number of seats); building codes and accessibility (ramps, door widths, escape routes) always win.

## Experiences

Sessions, courses, retreats and events.

| What | Aligned |
|---|---|
| Sessions | 36, 54, 72 or 108 minutes |
| Group sizes | 3, 6, 9, 12, 18, 36, 108 |
| Programmes | 9 days, 21 days, 36 days, **108 days** (a full cycle of change), 6 or 9 months |
| Steps in a journey | 3, 6 or 9 |
| Rhythm | in tune with the seasons and the moon: begin in spring or at a new moon, celebrate in summer or at a full moon, review in autumn, rest in winter |

## Aligned Dates

Following the [Law of Rhythm](universal-laws.md), a project is planned and carried out with supporting energies: its key dates are chosen with the moon and the positions of the planets and stars.

### The Moon

Events, launches and programmes start on a moon day that fits what they are meant to do.

| Phase | Energy | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| 🌑 New moon | seeding, intention | beginnings: a launch, a first session, signing, setting intentions |
| 🌒 Waxing moon | growth, building up | inviting, building, promoting, gathering people |
| 🌕 Full moon | fullness, visibility | celebrations, openings, showing results, gratitude |
| 🌘 Waning moon | release, clearing | reviewing, finishing, letting go, cleaning up, rest |

### The Stars

For the dates that matter most (a launch, an opening, a contract, the start of a programme), look at the astrological positions as well: for example, avoid starting something new while Mercury is retrograde, and choose a day whose sign suits the project. Consult an astrologer for the important ones.

### Encoded Materials

Flyers, invitations and printed materials carry the code of their moment: the date in aligned numbers where it's ours to set, the moon phase or its symbol, sacred geometry (see [Symbols](4-symbols.md)) and, quietly in the background, a Grabovoi sequence that fits the intention (see [Numbers](1-numbers.md#grabovoi-sequences)).

Dates are a free choice only where they are ours: a client's deadline, a public holiday or the law keeps its date.
