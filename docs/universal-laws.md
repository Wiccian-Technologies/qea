# ✦ Everything Is Energy · The Seven Universal Laws

∞ ∞ ∞

QEA respects the seven universal laws that existed long before it. All seven express one primary law, the one quantum physics points to:

> **Everything is energy.**

What looks solid (a table, a house, a book) is, at its smallest scale, energy and information in motion. The seven laws describe how that energy behaves. We call them laws because they work everywhere, for everyone, all the time. QEA doesn't add to them; it builds in harmony with them.

| | Law | In one line | In QEA |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Perpetual Transmutation of Energy | Energy is always changing form; change is all there is. | Build for change. |
| 2 | Vibration | Everything vibrates; like attracts like. | Choose the frequency on purpose. |
| 3 | Relativity | Things gain meaning only in relation to something else. | Never rank or compare people. |
| 4 | Polarity | Every thing holds its opposite. | Turn the heavy into the light. |
| 5 | Rhythm | Everything swings and flows in cycles. | Follow seasons and moon. |
| 6 | Cause and Effect | Every effect has a cause; every cause has an effect. | Intention first. |
| 7 | Gender | Creation needs both poles and its own gestation time. | Balance, and give things time. |

## 1 ✦ The Law of Perpetual Transmutation of Energy

Energy is always moving and changing. It takes one form, then another, and never stands still. Even what seems most stable, a stone or a skyscraper, is in constant change at its smallest scale. Change isn't something that happens now and then: change is all there is.

**In QEA:** we build for change. Values live in tokens and configuration, not scattered through the work, so a product can transform without breaking. Nothing is finished; everything is in its current form.

## 2 ✦ The Law of Vibration

Everything in the universe, seen and unseen, from a thought to a mountain, vibrates at its own frequency. Thought is one of the highest and most potent forms of energy: every thought sends out a vibration.

Its subsidiary, the **Law of Attraction**, tells us that like attracts like. Two drops of water merge; a drop of oil and a drop of water stay apart. We draw to us what we focus on and feed with energy: *what you think about, you bring about.* Think about your dreams and you move towards them; dwell on your fears and you move towards those. The choice is ours.

Most of us have felt it: the air in a room full of stress, or how it changes when someone walks in.

**In QEA:** every choice carries a frequency, so we choose it on purpose. Numbers in 3 · 6 · 9, colours with aligned hex digits, high-vibration words, music at 432 Hz. A product built from one clear intention attracts the people who share it. This law is the root of the [Nine Dimensions](principles.md).

## 3 ✦ The Law of Relativity

Everything in the material world is made real by its relation to something else. Everything "just is" until we compare it. Nothing has meaning except the meaning we give it.

How we compare either lifts us up or holds us down. Let the comparison help you find the good, and it lifts.

**In QEA:** we never rank or compare people in a product: no leaderboards, no "better than". We show each thing in relation to its own growth, and we give meaning deliberately through words, colour and symbols (see [Words](5-words.md)).

## 4 ✦ The Law of Polarity

Nothing exists without its opposite. Where there is hate, love exists too. Where we see low potential, high potential is also there. Every "failure" carries the seeds of success. And the opposites are two ends of the same thing: hot and cold are both temperature.

Knowing that every challenge holds the seed of its opposite turns life into learning and adventure.

**In QEA:** an error, an empty page, a "no" each hold their opposite. An empty state shows the next step; an error says kindly what to do; a heavy word is turned into a light one with ||∿ (see [Words](5-words.md)). Light and dark themes are one design.

## 5 ✦ The Law of Rhythm

Energy moves like a pendulum: whatever swings to the right swings back to the left. Everything is part of a dance, flowing and swinging back and forth. Everything is either growing or letting go. The better we understand this rhythm, the better we see what a moment asks for.

**In QEA:** we time things with the natural cycles: the seasons and their elements (see [Elements & Seasons](3-elements-seasons.md)) and the phases of the moon (see [Aligned Dates](9-products-spaces.md#aligned-dates)). Motion in a product is calm and breathes in and out; work has times of growth and times of rest.

## 6 ✦ The Law of Cause and Effect

Every effect has a cause, and every cause has an effect. Each cause is itself the effect of something before it, and each effect becomes the cause of what comes next: a never-ending cycle. Nothing happens by accident. When something succeeds, it has a cause; find which law was at work, and you can apply it again.

**In QEA:** intention is the first cause. Every piece starts with one sentence saying whom it serves (see [How to Apply It](principles.md#how-to-apply-it)), and every exception says why (`qea-ignore`). When something works, we write down why, so it can work again.

## 7 ✦ The Law of Gender

This law governs creation. Masculine and feminine, yin and yang, must unite for anything to be created, and everything has its own incubation or gestation period.

Nothing new is ever really created or destroyed: what seems new is something that already existed, changing form. All the good we wish for already exists, from the one source everything is made of; our part is to tune in to it and let it take form. Like radio waves filling a room unseen, it is already here: tune in to the right station and stay there. Faith is trusting the unseen: believe it until you see it.

And everything has its gestation period, which we don't always know. Many give up just before the seed breaks through the ground. Plant the seed, have faith and wait. What you are seeking is also seeking you.

**In QEA:** a design is balanced between the feminine and the masculine (soft and strong, curves and lines, warm and cool), so it speaks to everyone. And we give things their time: programmes of 9, 36 or **108 days**, launches on [aligned dates](9-products-spaces.md#aligned-dates), and patience between planting and harvest.
