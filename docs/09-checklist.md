# 9 ✦ Checklist

∞ ∞ ∞

Before something goes out (a page, a release, a presentation, a product), go through these nine.

1. **Numbers.** Every free size, spacing, duration, limit and count divides by 3, ideally by 9. `node tools/qea-check.mjs <folder>` reports nothing, or only lines marked `qea-ignore` with a reason.
2. **Colours.** Every colour comes from the palette and has a role and a feeling; meanings checked for the audience's culture; never colour alone; every new colour's hex digits sum to a multiple of 3; text meets 4.5 : 1 contrast.
3. **Standards.** Accessibility, protocols, the law and third-party values were kept, not forced.
4. **Elements & seasons.** Grouped content follows 🜃 🜄 🜂 🜁 ✧; the dominant element is balanced; seasonal colours fit the season where the audience lives.
5. **Symbols.** ✦ frames the main call to action; ∞ ∞ ∞ and ✧ are used sparingly; no yellow smiley faces; every symbol renders (drawn where a font can't).
6. **Words.** High-vibration, heart-based wording; no health or money promises from practices; heavy words turned with ||∿ where named; keywords consistent; everyone's own words respected.
7. **Structure.** Headings in title case; content in threes, sixes or nines where it fits; one clear main action per screen or page.
8. **Media.** Images and films compressed, in aligned sizes and lengths; films have a still, a pause and captions where there is speech.
9. **Intention.** The piece says, in one sentence, whom it serves, and that sentence is true.

✦ When all nine are met, it is aligned. ✦
