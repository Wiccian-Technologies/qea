# ✦ Testing Resonance

∞ ∞ ∞

The tools check what can be counted: numbers and colours. Resonance is felt. Before something goes out, we also ask: does it feel right, light and true? Here are ways to find out, from the simplest to the most precise.

## Feel It Yourself

Sit with the piece for a moment, breathe and notice. Does your body open or tighten? Does it feel light or heavy? Compare two versions side by side: the one that feels lighter usually is.

## Muscle Testing

Muscle testing (from kinesiology) uses the body's response to compare options: the muscle stays strong for what resonates and weakens for what doesn't. Test two versions of a logo, a colour or a text and keep the stronger one. Learn it from a trained practitioner, and test calmly and without wanting a certain answer.

## Dowsing

With a pendulum or dowsing rods, a trained dowser can test options, materials and places, for example where a desk or a bed is best placed (see [Architecture](9a-architecture.md)).

## Bioresonance

Bioresonance technology, such as Wiccian's own and TimeWaver, analyses and balances the frequencies of a person, a project, a product or a place. Wiccian uses it to test how a brand, a product or a space resonates, and to align it further. Ask [Wiccian](https://wiccian.io) for a resonance test.

## Ask the People

The people a product serves are the truest test. Show them, listen to how they feel, and watch how they use it. Their feedback counts more than any rule.

## Keep in Mind

Resonance testing is a practice, not a scientific measurement (see [Practices, Not Promises](principles.md#practices-not-promises)). When a test and a standard disagree (contrast, safety, the law), the standard wins.
