# 8 ✦ Images, Films & Products

∞ ∞ ∞

Your QEO code is woven into your communication and branding, mostly invisibly, and carries your frequency out into the world.

## Images

| What | Aligned |
|---|---|
| Longest edge | 144 (avatars, icons), 432 (logos, cards), 720, 1080, 1296, 1440 (photos), 2160 (large backgrounds) |
| Display sizes | from the aligned scale: 18, 36, 54, 72, 108, 144 … |
| Shapes | square, circle, 3 : 4, 9 : 16 for portraits; 16 : 9 for wide (standard) |
| Weight | photos under 864 KB, logos under 540 KB, after compression |
| Format | WebP; PNG for transparent logos; JPEG where a tool needs it (link previews) |

Every image is compressed before it is stored and shown at the size it appears.

## Films

| What | Aligned |
|---|---|
| Background films | 960 px wide, about 1.44 Mbit/s, muted and looping, with a still for Reduce Motion and a pause button |
| Story films | 720 px longest edge, about 0.9 Mbit/s video and 96 kbit/s sound, captions where there is speech |
| Lengths | clips of 9, 18, 36 or 54 seconds; stories up to 108 or 144 seconds |
| Format | MP4 (H.264 + AAC): plays on every phone |

Standards keep their size: 1920 × 1080 masters, 1200 × 630 link previews.

## Quiet Integration

Symbols, numbers and colours of a client's QEO code can be integrated so they are felt more than seen: the geometry inside an icon, a texture in a background, the proportions of a layout, a film's length. Integration never harms legibility, accessibility or the product's purpose.

## Products

Physical and digital products can carry the same code: aligned dimensions and quantities, palette colours, symbols on packaging or in the interface, an intention in the documentation. The rules of the other dimensions apply; standards and safety requirements always win.
