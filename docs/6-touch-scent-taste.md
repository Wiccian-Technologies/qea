# 6 ✦ Touch, Scent & Taste

∞ ∞ ∞

Sight, sound and words reach people from afar. Touch, scent and taste reach them up close, and are often what they remember most. In products, spaces, retreats and events, these senses carry the same intention as everything else.

## Touch

- **Materials by element:** wood and linen for Wood, clay, stone and wool for Earth, metal and glass for Metal, smooth and flowing fabrics for Water, warm, textured surfaces for Fire (see [Elements](7-elements.md)).
- **Natural first:** wood, stone, clay, cotton, linen, wool, paper with texture.
- **Pleasant to hold:** soft edges and rounded corners, a good weight, surfaces that feel warm rather than cold.
- **Print:** uncoated or recycled paper, embossing for symbols that are felt more than seen.

## Scent

- **Natural scents only:** pure essential oils, fresh flowers, herbs, wood and resin (palo santo, copal, sage, where they are sustainably sourced). No synthetic fragrances.
- **By element and season:** citrus and fresh green in spring, flowers and spices in summer, vanilla and cinnamon in late summer, cedar and eucalyptus in autumn, frankincense and sandalwood in winter.
- **Gently:** a light scent that welcomes, never one that fills the room. Ask about allergies, and leave a scent-free space where people gather.

## Taste

- **Natural and seasonal:** local, organic, plant-rich food and drinks of the season.
- **Balanced by the five tastes**, each linked to an element in Chinese tradition: sour (Wood), bitter (Fire), sweet (Earth), pungent (Metal), salty (Water).
- **Water and tea:** clean water, herbal teas and cacao for ceremonies and gatherings.
- **Care for everyone:** label what's in the food and offer options for allergies and diets.

## Aligned Counts

Where they're free: 3, 6 or 9 dishes, scents or materials in a set; tastings and ceremonies of 36, 54 or 108 minutes (see [Numbers & Proportion](1-numbers-proportion.md)). Food safety and labelling rules always win.
