# ✦ Glossary

∞ ∞ ∞

The words of QEA, in short.

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **3 · 6 · 9** | The core numbers of QEA. A free number is aligned when it divides by 3, ideally by 9. |
| **432 Hz** | A tuning of the note A, used in QEA for music where it's ours to choose (the standard is 440 Hz). |
| **Aligned** | Chosen in resonance with QEA's rules: 3 · 6 · 9, the colour rule, the elements and the seasons. |
| **Aligned date** | A date chosen with the moon and the stars to support what begins on it. |
| **Bagua** | Feng Shui's 3 × 3 map of the areas of life, laid over a plan, a room or a page. |
| **Bazi** | Chinese astrology's "four pillars" of a person's birth, showing their elements. |
| **BG3** | In BioGeometry, the balancing energy quality found in the centre of living systems and harmonious shapes. |
| **BioGeometry** | Dr. Ibrahim Karim's practice of balancing energy through shape (see [Spaces & Architecture](apply-spaces.md)). |
| **Bioresonance** | Technology that analyses and balances the frequencies of a person, project or place, such as Wiccian's (offered through [HerzEnergy](https://herzworld.one/herzenergy)) and TimeWaver. |
| **Brahmasthan** | In Vastu, the open, quiet centre of a building. |
| **Building biology (Baubiologie)** | The study of how buildings affect our health: natural materials, clean air, little electrosmog. |
| **Coherence** | When every part of something comes from one intention and fits together. |
| **Dowsing** | Finding answers, water or earth energies with a pendulum or rods. |
| **Elements** | QEA's five (Earth, Water, Fire, Air, Ether) and the five of Feng Shui, Wu Xing (Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water). |
| **Form, Vibration, Nature** | QEA's three families of dimensions: what is seen, what is heard and felt, and which element, when and where. |
| **Feng Shui** | China's art of placing things in harmony with the flow of energy (qi). |
| **Geomancy** | Reading the land and its earth energies before building. |
| **Golden ratio** | The proportion 1 : 1.618, found throughout nature and sacred architecture. |
| **Grabovoi sequence** | A number sequence by Grigori Grabovoi held for an intention; used as written, never rounded. |
| **Hartmann and Curry grids** | Networks of earth energy lines described in geomancy. |
| **Intention** | The one sentence of whom something serves and how it should feel. |
| **Lotus Flower of Life** | Circles of one size turning around one shared centre, 12-fold, like a blossom. QEA uses it instead of the Flower of Life (see [Shapes & Symbols](3-shapes-symbols.md#the-lotus-flower-of-life)). |
| **Muscle testing** | Using the body's muscle response to compare options (from kinesiology). |
| **New Homeopathy** | Erich Körbler's practice of balancing energy with simple signs such as lines and the sinus curve. |
| **qea-ignore** | A comment in code that marks a standard value the check should skip, with its reason. |
| **Rule of thirds** | Dividing a page, photo or wall into a 3 × 3 grid and placing what matters on its lines. |
| **Sacred geometry** | Forms and proportions held sacred across cultures: the circle, the Lotus Flower of Life, the golden ratio. |
| **Solfeggio frequencies** | Nine tones used in sound healing, such as 528 Hz. |
| **Token** | A named value in code (a size, a colour, a duration) defined once and used everywhere. |
| **Vortex mathematics** | Marko Rodin's way of seeing numbers by their digit sums: 1 2 4 8 7 5 as a circuit, 3 and 6 as an axis, 9 as the still centre (see [Numbers & Proportion](1-numbers-proportion.md#why-9-vortex-mathematics)). |
| **Vastu Purusha Mandala** | Vastu's 9 × 9 grid of 81 squares laid over a plan, with an open centre (see [Place & Direction](9-place-direction.md#the-vastu-mandala)). |
| **Vastu Shastra** | India's ancient science of architecture, aligned with the directions and the elements. |
| **✦ ✧ ∞ ∞ ∞ ∞ \|\|∿ ♥ ☉ ☽ and the spiral** | QEA's nine core symbols: the star, the open star, the divider, the single infinity, the lines and sinus curve from New Homeopathy, the heart, the sun, the moon with its phases, and the spiral turning to the left (see [Shapes & Symbols](3-shapes-symbols.md#core-symbols)). |
