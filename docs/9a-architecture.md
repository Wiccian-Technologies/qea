# Architecture

∞ ∞ ∞

A building carries a frequency long before anyone lives in it. Its land, direction, proportions and materials are chosen once and felt for decades. This page brings QEA into architecture, best while a building is still being planned, when every choice is still free. Building codes, structural and fire safety and accessibility always win.

## The Traditions We Draw On

QEA architecture brings several old and new traditions together. Each one is a part of the whole; none stands alone.

| Tradition | In short |
|---|---|
| **Feng Shui** | The five elements, their directions and the Bagua, placed in a building (see [Elements & Seasons](3-elements-seasons.md) and [Feng Shui for Brands & Business](3a-feng-shui-business.md)) |
| **Vastu Shastra** | India's ancient science of building: directions, the 9 × 9 mandala of a plan and an open, quiet centre |
| **Sacred geometry** | Harmonious proportions such as the golden ratio, and the forms of [Symbols](4-symbols.md) |
| **Geomancy** | Reading the land and its earth energies before anything is built |
| **Building biology (Baubiologie)** | Natural materials, healthy air and light, and little electrosmog |
| **BioGeometry** | Shape and a balanced centre bring harmony (see [below](#biogeometry)) |

## BioGeometry

BioGeometry was founded in the 1970s by Dr. Ibrahim Karim, an Egyptian architect trained at ETH Zurich. It describes itself as *"the science of using the energy principles of shape to qualitatively balance biological energy systems."* It is one of the traditions on this page. What follows are its own principles, in short.

### Its Core Principles

- **Shape carries energy.** Every form, from a line or an angle to a room or a whole building, gives off a subtle energy quality through its shape alone.
- **The centre brings balance.** BioGeometry describes one balancing quality in the centre of every living system and every harmonious shape. It calls it **BG3**, because it shows up as three qualities together: negative green, a higher harmonic of ultraviolet and a higher harmonic of gold.
- **A BioGeometry shape holds all three.** Only a shape that brings out BG3 counts as a BioGeometry shape. Such a shape is meant to strengthen the centre of the living systems around it.
- **Harmony lives in proportion.** Proportion, above all the golden ratio, is seen as the measure of harmony and beauty, as in sacred architecture.
- **Quality, not only quantity.** BioGeometry calls its basis the *Physics of Quality*: it looks at how energy feels and acts, not only at how much of it there is, and brings science and spirituality together.
- **Balance the environment.** It works with the energy of the earth (energy grids, geopathic stress) and of technology (phones, computers, wireless networks), aiming to bring their effect into balance rather than remove them.

### Where QEA Applies It

| Area | How |
|---|---|
| **Digital products** | Give every screen a clear, calm centre. Use golden ratio proportions alongside the 3 · 6 · 9 scale. Prefer balanced, whole shapes and gentle curves over crowded, sharp ones. |
| **Videos and photos** | Compose around a clear centre or on golden ratio lines. Keep shapes in the frame balanced and movement calm; choose natural light where you can (see [Images & Films](7-images-films.md)). |
| **Physical spaces** | Plan the building's shape, proportions and centre from the first sketch. Keep the centre of the building and of each room open and calm. Look at the land's earth energies before building. |
| **Offices** | Place desks and beds away from stress lines and away from routers and wiring. Choose centred, balanced room shapes and natural materials. Take breaks from screens. |

### Keep in Mind

- BioGeometry's shapes and products are its own and patented. QEA borrows the principles as design language. For the real shapes and a full balancing of a building or office, work with BioGeometry or a practitioner trained by them.
- These are BioGeometry's own principles, not scientifically established facts. Never promise health, protection or money effects (see [Practices, Not Promises](principles.md#practices-not-promises)).
