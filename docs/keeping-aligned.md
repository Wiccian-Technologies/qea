# ✦ Keeping It Aligned

∞ ∞ ∞

Following the [Law of Perpetual Transmutation](universal-laws.md), energy never stands still. A product, a space or a brand that was aligned at its launch drifts with time. A gentle rhythm keeps it aligned.

## A Rhythm of Care

| When | What |
|---|---|
| **Every day** | A clear desk and a calm start (see [Before You Create](before-you-create.md)) |
| **Every moon cycle** | At the waning moon: tidy files, close what is finished, let go of what doesn't serve |
| **Every season** | Change the seasonal palette, photographs and offers; run the [review of all nine](checklist.md) again |
| **Every year** | Look at the whole: is the intention still true? Does the brand's element still fit? Retire what no longer serves |

## Spaces

- **Clear them** at the change of each season: open the windows, clean, declutter, and refresh plants, flowers and water.
- **Space clearing** with sound (bells, singing bowls, drums), natural incense or simply fresh air after big events, moves or difficult times.
- **Move what's stuck:** a corner nobody uses or a pile that grows asks for attention.

## Digital Products

- **Declutter:** remove unused pages, features, files and code; fewer things, each one cared for.
- **Check alignment:** run `npx qea-check src/` over everything now and then, and update the palette or tokens when something crept in. This is the safety net; the real alignment happens while building (see [Build It In First](apply-qea.md#build-it-in-first)).
- **Listen:** read what people say each season, and let the product grow with them.

## Retire with Gratitude

When something has done its work (a product, an offer, a page), close it with care: thank it, tell the people it served, and make room for what comes next.
