# Spaces & Architecture

∞ ∞ ∞

A building carries a frequency long before anyone lives in it. Its land, direction, proportions and materials are chosen once and felt for decades. This page brings QEA into shops, studios, offices, homes and venues, and into architecture, best while a building is still being planned, when every choice is still free. Building codes, structural and fire safety and accessibility always win.

## Any Space

For a room that already exists, as much as for a new building:

- **Place the elements** by their directions and keep the centre open (see [Place & Direction](9-place-direction.md)).
- **Light:** daylight first; warm light for rest and welcome, neutral light for work (see [Colours & Light](2-colours-light.md#light)).
- **Sound:** music tuned to 432 Hz at a calm volume, or the sounds of the season (see [Sound & Music](5-sound-music.md)).
- **Measurements:** aligned where they are free (shelf heights, table sizes, the number of seats); building codes and accessibility (ramps, door widths, escape routes) always win.

## The Traditions We Draw On

QEA architecture brings several traditions together. Each one is a part of the whole, and none comes first.

### Feng Shui

China's art of placing a building, and everything in it, in harmony with the flow of energy (qi). It works with the five elements, their directions and the Bagua, a 3 × 3 map of the areas of life laid over a plan. QEA uses it to place colours, materials and shapes where their element lives (see [Elements](7-elements.md) and [Brands & Business](apply-brands.md)).

### Vastu Shastra

India's ancient science of architecture. It aligns a building with the directions, the sun and the five elements, and lays a 9 × 9 grid, the Vastu Purusha Mandala, over the plan, with an open, quiet centre (the Brahmasthan). QEA uses it for orientation, the placing of rooms and a free centre. Where its directions differ from Feng Shui, choose one tradition for a building and follow it throughout.

### Sacred Geometry

The proportions and forms found throughout nature and in temples of many cultures: the golden ratio, the circle, the square, the Flower of Life. QEA uses them for the proportions of plans, rooms, windows and details, alongside the 3 · 6 · 9 scale (see [Shapes & Symbols](3-shapes-symbols.md) and [Numbers & Proportion](1-numbers-proportion.md)).

### Geomancy

The old art, known in Europe and many other cultures, of reading the land before building: its water, its earth energies (such as the Hartmann and Curry grids) and its places of strength or stress, often found by dowsing. QEA uses it to choose the site, where the building stands and where people sleep and work.

### Building Biology (Baubiologie)

Born in Germany in the 1970s, it sees a building as our third skin and asks how it affects the people inside. It favours natural, breathable materials, clean air, healthy humidity, daylight, quiet and little electrosmog, and has its own guidelines for measuring them. QEA uses it for materials, wiring and the indoor climate.

### BioGeometry

Founded in the 1970s by Dr. Ibrahim Karim, an Egyptian architect, it describes itself as *"the science of using the energy principles of shape to qualitatively balance biological energy systems."* In its view every shape carries an energy quality, and a balanced centre, which it calls BG3, brings harmony to the living systems around it. QEA uses it for the shape, the proportions and the centre of buildings, rooms and objects.

## Through the Life of a Building

| Phase | What we align |
|---|---|
| **The land** | Its earth energies, water and slope; the direction the building will face |
| **The design** | Orientation, zones and the centre; aligned measurements; proportions; elements and materials |
| **The building** | Natural, healthy materials; wiring planned with care; key steps such as breaking ground on [aligned dates](8-time-rhythm.md#aligned-dates) |
| **Moving in** | An opening on an aligned date; light, sound and plants that balance the elements |

## Where QEA Applies Them

| Area | How |
|---|---|
| **Digital products** | Give every screen a clear, calm centre. Use golden ratio proportions alongside the 3 · 6 · 9 scale. Balance the elements in colour and shape; prefer whole shapes and gentle curves over crowded, sharp ones. |
| **Videos and photos** | Compose around a clear centre or on golden ratio lines. Keep the elements in the frame balanced and movement calm; choose natural light where you can (see [Images & Films](apply-images-films.md)). |
| **Physical spaces** | Read the land first. Orient and zone the plan, keep the centre open and quiet, place each element where it lives, and build with natural materials. |
| **Offices** | Place desks away from stress lines, routers and wiring. Bring in daylight, plants and natural materials, keep the elements in balance, and take breaks from screens. |

## Keep in Mind

- Several of these traditions have trained practitioners and their own tools, and some protect their shapes (BioGeometry's are patented). For a full reading or balancing of a site or building, work with a practitioner of that tradition.
- These are practices, not scientifically established facts. Never promise health, protection or money effects (see [Practices, Not Promises](principles.md#practices-not-promises)).
