# Products & Print

∞ ∞ ∞

Everything a brand offers can carry the same code: the product in the hand, the card in the post, the flyer on the wall. The nine dimensions apply here together; standards, safety rules and legal labels always win.

## Physical Products

- **Dimensions and quantities:** 9 × 9 cm cards, 36 or 108 pieces in a pack, 54 ml or 108 ml bottles, 3 or 9 items in a set.
- **Colours:** from the brand's aligned palette; a season's palette for a seasonal edition (see [A Palette for Each Season](8-time-rhythm.md#a-palette-for-each-season)).
- **Shapes:** chosen by element: circles and arches for Metal, squares for Earth, waves for Water, points for Fire, tall forms for Wood.
- **Symbols:** on packaging, inside a lid, embossed or printed quietly (see [Shapes & Symbols](3-shapes-symbols.md)).
- **Intention:** one sentence in the documentation or on the inside of the packaging.
- **Standards win:** paper sizes (A4, A5), shipping and safety rules, legal labels and measurements.

## Print

- **Sizes:** standard paper sizes (A4, A5, A6) stay as they are; margins and spacing inside them come from the aligned scale.
- **Paper:** uncoated or recycled, pleasant to touch (see [Touch, Scent & Taste](6-touch-scent-taste.md) and [Care for the Earth](care-for-the-earth.md)).
- **Layout:** the main message in the centre, the next step near the bottom (see [The Bagua](9-place-direction.md#the-bagua)).

## Encoded Materials

Flyers, invitations and printed materials carry the code of their moment: the date in aligned numbers where it's ours to set, the moon phase or its symbol, sacred geometry (see [Shapes & Symbols](3-shapes-symbols.md)) and, quietly in the background, a Grabovoi sequence that fits the intention (see [Numbers](1-numbers-proportion.md#grabovoi-sequences)).

The moment itself is chosen on [Time & Rhythm](8-time-rhythm.md#aligned-dates).
