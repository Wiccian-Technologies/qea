# Experiences & Events

∞ ∞ ∞

Sessions, courses, retreats, ceremonies and gatherings are lived through time, with all the senses. Here the nine dimensions meet in one moment.

| What | Aligned |
|---|---|
| Sessions | 36, 54, 72 or 108 minutes |
| Group sizes | 3, 6, 9, 12, 18, 36, 108 |
| Programmes | 9, 21 or 36 days, **108 days** (a full cycle of change), 6 or 9 months |
| Steps in a journey | 3, 6 or 9 |
| Start dates | a new moon, the start of a season (see [Aligned Dates](8-time-rhythm.md#aligned-dates)) |

## The Whole Experience

- **The place:** a calm, strong place, the elements in balance (see [Place & Direction](9-place-direction.md)).
- **The light:** daylight, sunrise or warm evening light (see [Colours & Light](2-colours-light.md#light)).
- **Sound:** silence, the sounds of nature, music at 432 Hz (see [Sound & Music](5-sound-music.md)).
- **Touch, scent and taste:** natural materials, a gentle natural scent, local seasonal food and herbal teas (see [Touch, Scent & Taste](6-touch-scent-taste.md)).
- **Words:** a warm, short invitation; no pressure (see [Words](4-words.md)).
- **The Earth:** low waste, and the place left better than it was found (see [Care for the Earth](care-for-the-earth.md)).

## Holding the Space

Open with arrival (a few breaths, the intention spoken), give the middle its full time, and close with gratitude. Begin and end on time: a held rhythm creates trust.

Worked examples: [a Retreat Space](../examples/retreat-space.md) and [an Event Flyer](../examples/event-flyer.md).
