# ✦ Before You Create

∞ ∞ ∞

Everything we make carries the frequency of the person who makes it. Following the [Law of Cause and Effect](universal-laws.md), the work begins inside us: a product built in calm and joy carries calm and joy. So before we align the numbers and colours, we align ourselves.

## The Maker

- **Arrive.** Take three to nine slow breaths before you begin, and feel your heart.
- **Hold the intention.** Read the one sentence of whom this work serves (see [How to Apply It](principles.md#how-to-apply-it)), and feel it, not only think it.
- **Create in coherence.** Work from love and curiosity rather than pressure or fear. When you notice stress, pause, breathe, move, drink water, step outside.
- **Rest.** Tired hands make heavy work. Work in rhythms (for example 54 or 108 minutes, then a break) and stop when the day is done.

## The Space

- **Clear it.** A tidy desk and a clean screen: close what you don't need.
- **Bring in nature.** Daylight, fresh air, a plant, water to drink.
- **Set the tone.** Silence, or quiet music tuned to 432 Hz (see [Sound & Music](6-sound-music.md)).

## The Team

- **Begin together.** Open a project or a meeting with a moment of silence or a shared intention.
- **Speak from the heart.** Honest, kind and clear (see [Words](5-words.md)); every voice is heard.
- **Close together.** End with gratitude for what was made, and let go of what didn't work.

A small daily practice (meditation, breathing, movement) keeps the maker aligned, and with the maker, the work.
