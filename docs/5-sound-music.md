# 5 ✦ Sound & Music

∞ ∞ ∞

Sound reaches the body before the mind: a tone can calm the breath or raise the pulse before a word is understood. QEA aligns the frequencies, rhythms and lengths of every sound a brand makes, from a notification chime to a film's soundtrack, a podcast, a shop's music or a meditation.

## Tuning ✦ 432 Hz

Most music today is tuned to **A = 440 Hz** (the ISO 16 concert pitch). QEA prefers **A = 432 Hz** for music a brand commissions or chooses. Many listeners describe it as warmer, softer and more relaxed; 432 itself is a master number (4 + 3 + 2 = 9, and 432 = 3 × 144).

- **Recording new music:** ask the musicians to tune to A = 432 Hz.
- **Existing music:** shifting 440 Hz music to 432 Hz lowers it by about **31.77 cents** (a third of a semitone). Use pitch shifting without changing the tempo, in any audio editor.
- **Standards win:** an orchestra, a choir or a live band tunes as it does; a licensed track stays as it was released unless the licence allows changes.

## Brainwave Ranges

Rhythmic sound (binaural beats on headphones, isochronic pulses, slow drumming) can invite a state of mind:

| State | Range | Serves |
|---|---|---|
| Delta | 0.5–4 Hz | deep sleep |
| Theta | 4–8 Hz | meditation, deep relaxation, creativity |
| Alpha | 8–12 Hz | calm focus, learning, the "flow" of a relaxed mind |
| Beta | 12–30 Hz | alert thinking, everyday activity |

The Earth's own resonance, the **Schumann resonance**, lies at about **7.83 Hz**, at the border of theta and alpha: often used as the base of grounding sessions.

## Sound in Products

- **Silence is the default.** Sound asks for attention: interface sounds are few, soft, short and optional, and every one can be turned off.
- **Respect the device:** follow the phone's mute switch and system volume; never autoplay sound. Films start muted with captions on.
- **Never sound alone:** every sound signal also has something to see (a message, an icon, a vibration).
- **Calm loudness:** master music to about **−14 LUFS** for streaming and **−23 LUFS** for broadcast (the standards win); keep interface sounds well below the content.
- **Gentle edges:** fade in and out over 180–540 ms; no sudden peaks.
- **Standards win:** sample rates 44.1 or 48 kHz, broadcast loudness rules, licensed music as licensed.

Frequencies, tunings and brainwave effects are practices, not proven treatments: describe what a sound is meant to support, never promise healing (see [Principles](principles.md#practices-not-promises)).
