# ✦ Before It Goes Out

∞ ∞ ∞

A review of the nine dimensions before something goes out: a page, a release, a presentation, a product, a space or an event.

**First, always:** the piece says in one sentence whom it serves, and that sentence is true. It was made in a calm, clear state (see [Before You Create](before-you-create.md)), with care for the Earth (see [Care for the Earth](care-for-the-earth.md)). Accessibility, protocols, the law and third-party values were kept, not forced.

1. **Numbers.** Every free size, spacing, duration, limit and count divides by 3, ideally by 9. `node tools/qea-check.mjs <folder>` reports nothing, or only lines marked `qea-ignore` with a reason.
2. **Colours.** Every colour comes from the palette and has a role and a feeling; its hex digits sum to a multiple of 3; text meets 4.5 : 1 contrast; meanings checked for the audience's culture; never colour alone.
3. **Elements & Seasons.** Grouped content follows 🜃 🜄 🜂 🜁 ✧; the dominant element is balanced (by the weakening cycle before the controlling one); the brand's element fits its industry and owner; seasonal colours fit the season where the audience lives.
4. **Symbols.** ✦ frames the main call to action; ∞ ∞ ∞ and ✧ are used sparingly; no yellow smiley faces; every symbol renders (drawn where a font can't); traditions used with respect.
5. **Words.** High-vibration, heart-based wording; heavy words turned with ||∿ where named; keywords consistent; headings in title case; content in threes, sixes or nines where it fits; everyone's own words respected; no health or money promises from practices.
6. **Sound & Music.** Music at 432 Hz where it's ours to choose; sounds short, soft, optional and never autoplayed; lengths and tempos aligned; loudness within the standard.
7. **Images & Films.** Compressed, in aligned sizes and lengths; films have a still, a pause and captions where there is speech.
8. **Code.** Constants named and aligned; colours only from tokens; the palette test and `qea-check` run in CI; intention comments true and short.
9. **Products, Spaces & Experiences.** Dimensions, quantities, durations and group sizes aligned; elements placed and balanced; key dates chosen with the moon and the stars; for buildings, the land read, the centre open and materials natural and healthy; touch, scent and taste chosen with care; safety and building rules kept.

**And finally:** it feels right, light and true (see [Testing Resonance](testing-resonance.md)).

✦ When all nine are met, it is aligned. ✦
