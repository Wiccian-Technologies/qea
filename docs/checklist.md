# ✦ Before It Goes Out

∞ ∞ ∞

A review of the nine dimensions before something goes out: a page, a release, a presentation, a product, a space or an event.

**First, always:** the piece says in one sentence whom it serves, and that sentence is true. It was made in a calm, clear state (see [Before You Create](before-you-create.md)), with care for the Earth (see [Care for the Earth](care-for-the-earth.md)). Accessibility, protocols, the law and third-party values were kept, not forced.

**I. Form**

1. **Numbers & Proportion.** Every free size, spacing, limit and count divides by 3, ideally by 9; proportions follow the rule of thirds or the golden ratio. `npx qea-check src/` says **✦ All aligned.** (standards kept with `qea-ignore` and a reason).
2. **Colours & Light.** Every colour comes from the palette and has a role and a feeling; its hex digits sum to a multiple of 3; text meets 4.5 : 1 contrast; meanings checked for the audience's culture; never colour alone; daylight first, warm or neutral light chosen for the purpose.
3. **Shapes & Symbols.** Shapes fit the element and feel whole and balanced; ✦ frames the main call to action; ∞ ∞ ∞ and ✧ are used sparingly; no yellow smiley faces; every symbol renders; traditions used with respect.

**II. Vibration**

4. **Words.** High-vibration, heart-based wording; heavy words turned with ||∿ where named; keywords consistent; headings in title case; content in threes, sixes or nines where it fits; everyone's own words respected; no health or money promises.
5. **Sound & Music.** Music at 432 Hz where it's ours to choose; sounds short, soft, optional and never autoplayed; lengths and tempos aligned; loudness within the standard.
6. **Touch, Scent & Taste.** Natural materials, gentle natural scents, local and seasonal food; allergies cared for.

**III. Nature**

7. **Elements.** Grouped content follows 🜃 🜄 🜂 🜁 ✧; the dominant element is balanced (by the weakening cycle before the controlling one); the brand's element fits its industry and owner.
8. **Time & Rhythm.** Durations aligned; key dates chosen with the season, the moon and the stars; seasonal colours fit the season where the audience lives.
9. **Place & Direction.** The place was read; elements placed by their directions; the centre kept open; for buildings, materials natural and healthy; building and safety rules kept.

**Where it lives:** for code, constants and colours come only from tokens and the check runs in CI; images and films are compressed, in aligned sizes, with captions and a pause; print is on kind paper.

**And finally:** it feels right, light and true (see [Testing Resonance](testing-resonance.md)).

✦ When all nine are met, it is aligned. ✦
