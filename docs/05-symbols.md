# 5 ✦ Symbols

∞ ∞ ∞

We use sacred geometry and power symbols from around the world, known for their beneficial vibration and protection, to hold a field around a person, a business and its work.

## The Wiccian Symbols

| Symbol | Name | Use |
|---|---|---|
| ✦ | Star | Frames a call to action on both sides (`✦ Get Quantum Power ✦`); separates a title from its subtitle (`Quantum Business Flow ✦ From Goals to Alignment`); marks list items and questions |
| ✧ | Open star | The softer accent: a signature after a name (`Meet Sarah ✧`), list items in calm texts |
| ∞ ∞ ∞ | Infinity | The divider under a section title or between sections |
| ♥ | Heart | Herz and everything given from the heart |
| \|\|∿ | Turning wave | Placed right after a heavy word or pattern, to turn and release it: `limiting beliefs ||∿`, `undesired influences ||∿` (see [Words](06-words.md)) |
| ∞ | Infinity (single) | Abundance, the unbroken flow |

## Sacred Geometry

Seed of Life, Flower of Life and other sacred shapes appear as backgrounds, films, placeholders and quiet ornaments. Example: the closing band of the Quantum Business Flow page shows the Seed of Life in motion.

## Elements

The alchemical signs 🜃 🜄 🜂 🜁 and ✧ name the five elements wherever they appear (see [Elements](04-elements.md)).

## Usage

- **Few and meaningful:** one ✦ pair per call to action, one ∞ ∞ ∞ per section at most.
- **Drawn or typed, never as emoji pictures:** prefer the typographic symbol or a drawn icon over colourful emoji. Never yellow smiley faces in interfaces.
- **Where a font can't draw a symbol** (link preview images, some PDFs), draw it as an SVG instead of leaving an empty box.
- **Quiet integration:** symbols may also live where they aren't seen at first glance (a texture, an icon's geometry, a code comment). See [Images, Films & Products](08-media.md).
