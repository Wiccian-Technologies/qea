# 8 ✦ Time & Rhythm

∞ ∞ ∞

Everything flows in cycles: the breath, the day, the moon, the seasons, the years (the [Law of Rhythm](universal-laws.md)). QEA aligns three things in time: **how long** something lasts, **when** it begins, and **which cycle** it follows. Every rhythm of QEA lives on this page.

## How Long

| What | Aligned |
|---|---|
| Interface motion | **180, 270, 360, 540, 720, 1080, 1440 ms**: calm, never flashy |
| Delays | 0, 504, 1008, 1512, 2016 ms when steps of half a second are wanted |
| Toasts, notices | 3, 6 or 9 seconds |
| Timeouts, retries | 3 tries; 9, 18 or 36 seconds |
| Code and link lifetimes | 9 minutes, 36 hours, 9 days, 30 days |
| Films and sound | see [Images & Films](apply-images-films.md) and [Sound & Music](5-sound-music.md#lengths) |
| Sessions, ceremonies | 36, 54, 72 or 108 minutes |
| Focused work | 54 or 108 minutes, then a break |
| Programmes | 9, 21 or 36 days, **108 days** (a full cycle of change), 6 or 9 months |

## The Day

- **Sunrise** is the time of beginnings: a morning practice, setting the intention, the first session of a retreat (see [Before You Create](before-you-create.md#daily-practices)).
- **Midday** is for full energy: meetings, making, being seen.
- **Evening** is for slowing down: warm light, quiet screens, reflection and gratitude.

## The Moon

Events, launches and programmes start on a moon day that fits what they are meant to do.

| Phase | Energy | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| ● New moon | seeding, intention | beginnings: a launch, a first session, signing, setting intentions |
| ◐ Waxing moon | growth, building up | inviting, building, promoting, gathering people |
| ○ Full moon | fullness, visibility | celebrations, openings, showing results, gratitude |
| ◑ Waning moon | release, clearing | reviewing, finishing, letting go, cleaning up, rest |

## The Seasons

Each season carries its element's energy. A brand, a campaign or a product can breathe with the year: a seasonal palette, a seasonal photograph, an offer that fits the season's energy, a release planned at its start.

| Season      | Element | Energy                    | Good for                         |
| ----------- | ------- | ------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Spring      | Wood    | awakening, growth         | launches, beginnings, learning   |
| Summer      | Fire    | fullness, joy, visibility | events, celebrations, being seen |
| Late summer | Earth   | harvest, nourishment      | gathering, gratitude, community  |
| Autumn      | Metal   | clarity, letting go       | reviews, simplifying, finishing  |
| Winter      | Water   | rest, depth               | reflection, planning, inner work |

### Seasons around the World

The five seasons follow the temperate north. Wherever you are, follow the rhythm of the place where your people live.

- **Southern hemisphere:** the seasons are reversed. Spring begins in September, summer in December, autumn in March, winter in June.
- **Tropics and near the equator:** there are often two seasons, wet and dry, rather than four. Map the local rhythm to the element whose energy fits:

| Local season | Energy | Element |
|---|---|---|
| Start of the rains | renewal, growth | Wood |
| Height of the dry, sunny season | fullness, visibility, celebration | Fire |
| Harvests | nourishment, gathering | Earth |
| End of a season, a clearing storm | letting go, clarity | Metal |
| Heavy rains, a quiet time | rest, depth, reflection | Water |

**An example, the Riviera Maya (Mexico):** the dry season (about November to April) is bright and full of visitors: a time for events and being seen (Fire). The rainy season (about May to October) brings growth and green (Wood), with a quieter, inward time at its height and during hurricane season (Water). Local festivals and harvests are moments of gathering (Earth).

- **Feng Shui directions:** in the classic tradition Fire lives in the south, where the sun stands at noon in the northern hemisphere. In the southern hemisphere some practitioners place Fire in the north, towards the sun, and Water in the south. Choose one way for a project and follow it throughout.
- **Local traditions first:** where a place has its own calendar and festivals (the Maya calendar, harvest feasts, the solstices), honour them alongside these.

## The Stars

For the dates that matter most (a launch, an opening, a contract, the start of a programme), look at the astrological positions as well: for example, avoid starting something new while Mercury is retrograde, and choose a day whose sign suits the project. Consult an astrologer for the important ones.

## Aligned Dates

Following the Law of Rhythm, a project is planned and carried out with supporting energies. Its key dates (a launch, an opening, a contract, the start of a programme, breaking ground for a building) are chosen with the moon, the season and the stars together:

1. **The season** sets the energy: begin in spring, celebrate in summer, gather in late summer, review in autumn, rest in winter (or the local rhythm where you live).
2. **The moon** sets the day: a new moon to begin, a full moon to celebrate.
3. **The stars** fine-tune the most important ones.
4. **Aligned numbers** where the date is ours to set, and a time such as 9:00, 9:09 or 15:33.

Printed materials can carry the code of their moment (see [Products & Print](apply-products-print.md#encoded-materials)).

Dates are a free choice only where they are ours: a client's deadline, a public holiday or the law keeps its date.

## Rhythms of Growth

Following the [Law of Gender](universal-laws.md), everything needs its own time to grow. Leave room between planting and harvest: a programme of 108 days, a launch prepared over a full moon cycle, a pause before the next big step. And keep things aligned over time with a [rhythm of care](keeping-aligned.md).
