# The Five Element Personalities

∞ ∞ ∞

Each of the five elements is also an archetype of character. Everyone carries all five, usually with one or two strong. Each archetype has its gifts and its challenges: knowing the gifts helps us see our own; understanding the challenges lets us work with those energies skilfully, so they don't get the best of us.

In QEA the archetypes help us understand the people we build for and with: how to speak to an audience, how a team works best together, and which element a founder brings to their brand (see [Brands & Business](apply-brands.md)).

| Element | Gift | Challenge | Balanced by |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Metal** 金 | clarity, vision, planning, leading, clear boundaries, order | perfectionism, unfinished projects, words that hurt | Water (drains it gently) |
| **Water** 水 | depth, thoughtfulness, insight beneath the surface | turning ideas into action | Wood (draws it out into growth) |
| **Wood** 木 | vision, imagination, will, a sense of justice, bold leadership | competing, arguing, needing to be right | Fire (lets it flow into warmth) |
| **Fire** 火 | joy, warmth, connection, compassion, enthusiasm | saying yes to too much, scattered, forgetting oneself | Earth (grounds it) |
| **Earth** 土 | holding space, nurture, authenticity, honesty, service | over-giving, caretaking until depleted | Metal (brings boundaries and clarity) |

## Metal: the Clear One

Metal personalities have a gift of clarity. They know what they want and trust they can make it happen, which makes them strong at planning and at leading others. Their clarity lets them set clear boundaries: if something feels out of alignment, they simply say no, without anger and without disrespect.

They are usually organised and tidy, because they hold an intention for how things should be. This serves them, and it can also turn into perfectionism that hurts themselves and others. Others may feel controlled or judged, projects may stay unfinished because nothing is quite perfect, and their candid opinions can wound.

**Working with Metal:** clear structure, precise information, quality. Invite them to call something finished when it is good.

## Water: the Deep One

Like a deep ocean or river, Water personalities look below the surface of what happens around them. They are exceptionally thoughtful and see what others overlook.

This inward depth makes them shy away from busy, noisy places. Rather than a big party, they choose a quiet conversation with one or two close friends at home. Their challenge is bringing their thoughts to life: even great ideas that could serve them and the world can stay unspoken.

**Working with Water:** calm spaces, time to think, small groups. Help them take the first small step from idea to action.

## Wood: the Visionary

Wood personalities are visionaries with wild imaginations and the will to match. When they decide to do something, they do it, whatever stands in the way, and so they achieve what others believed impossible.

They are fair-minded and driven by justice: they are moved when others are treated unfairly and ready to stand up for what is right. Many bold leaders are rich in Wood. Their challenge is to compete and argue, proving why they are right instead of seeking understanding and common ground.

**Working with Wood:** a vision worth growing towards, room to build, a cause that is just. Invite listening and finding the way together.

## Fire: the Radiant One

Fire personalities say "yes!" to life. They love to connect, to be among many people and at the centre of things. They are often kind, compassionate and caring, which makes them wonderful with people.

Their joy is infectious: on a hard day, their enthusiasm reminds us of the beauty the world has to offer. Their challenge is saying yes to too much, becoming overcommitted and scattered, and forgetting their own needs.

**Working with Fire:** celebration, connection, being seen. Help them choose a few things with their whole heart, and rest.

## Earth: the Nurturer

Earth personalities have a gift for holding space for others. They are nurturing, kind, authentic and honest, and make wonderful parents, partners and friends.

They often choose to serve, and many great healers are rich in Earth: they make others feel safe and held. Like Fire, they can overextend themselves; identifying too strongly as the caretaker, they forget their own needs and become depleted.

**Working with Earth:** warmth, trust, a stable place. Remind them that caring for themselves is part of caring for others.

## In a Product

- **Words:** speak to the element of your audience: precise and calm for Metal, deep and unhurried for Water, visionary for Wood, joyful for Fire, warm and safe for Earth (see [Words](4-words.md)).
- **Teams:** a team in balance carries all five: Wood starts, Fire connects, Earth holds, Metal finishes, Water reflects.
- **Never a box:** the archetypes describe energies, not people. Never sort, rank or label users by them.
