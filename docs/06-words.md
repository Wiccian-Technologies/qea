# 6 ✦ Words · Texts & Presentations

∞ ∞ ∞

Each word carries a positive or a negative charge. Masaru Emoto's water experiments are the image we work with: we are mostly water. So we use high-vibration, positive words in every communication and product, to attract the same.

## High-Vibration Language

- **Say what grows:** "clearer focus", "renewed energy", "aligned momentum", "flow", "ease", "abundance", "joy", "gratitude", "heart".
- **Name the wish, not the fear:** "Feel certain about your path" rather than "Stop feeling lost".
- **Heart-based selling:** speak to the person's longing and to what they will feel, user-oriented and honest; no pressure, no false scarcity, no countdowns.
- **Calm and clear:** short sentences, one idea each, the main action named once and plainly.

## Turning a Heavy Word

When a heavy word must be named (a fear, a blockage, a pattern), follow it with **||∿** to turn and release it:

> We start with reprogramming limiting beliefs ||∿ and clearing interfering frequency fields.

Use it sparingly, where the text names what is being released.

## Keywords

Choose a small set of resonant keywords for each brand or offer and weave them into headings, calls to action and product texts, so clients feel drawn to it and fall in love with the brand. Keep them consistent across the website, presentations and the product itself.

## Affirmations

An affirmation can close a page or a document, in the present tense and first person:

> I am a magnet for soul-aligned clients.

## Structure

- **Headings in title case** ("Questions before You Begin"), body text in sentence case.
- **Calls to action framed by ✦** (`✦ Start Your Journey ✦`).
- **Sections in three, six or nine parts** where content allows: three steps, six benefits, nine questions.
- **Element order** for anything grouped by element (see [Elements & Seasons](04-elements.md)).
- **Seasonal voice:** let campaigns speak with the season's energy (spring begins, summer celebrates, autumn simplifies, winter reflects).

## Everyone Keeps Their Own Words

Clients, partners and users speak in their own voice. QEA offers wording when it is asked for; it never rewrites someone's text against their will or forces one brand's vocabulary onto another's.
