# Applying QEA to a Retreat Space

∞ ∞ ∞

A worked example: a small retreat house near the sea, built with QEA while it is still being planned.

## Intention

> *"Guests arrive, breathe out and feel at home in themselves."*

The leading element is Earth (grounding, nourishment), balanced with Water (the sea, rest) and Wood (the garden, growth).

## The Land

Before anything is drawn, a geomancer reads the land: where water runs beneath it, where the earth energies are calm and where the strong places are. Bedrooms go on calm ground, the meditation room on a place of strength. The house faces the morning sun.

## The Design

- **Orientation and zones:** one tradition is chosen and followed throughout (here Vastu): the entrance in the east, the kitchen in the south-east, sleeping rooms in the south-west, the meditation room in the north-east.
- **An open centre:** a courtyard open to the sky, kept free and quiet (the Brahmasthan); a small fountain in the north-east, where Vastu places water.
- **Aligned measurements:** a 360 cm building grid, 270 cm ceilings, 9 guest rooms, a meditation room for 18 to 36 people.
- **Proportion:** windows and the courtyard in the golden ratio; arches and round forms in the meditation room.
- **Materials:** local stone, lime plaster, wood, clay tiles, linen; no plastic finishes.
- **Healthy building:** wiring routed away from the beds, a switch to turn off bedroom circuits at night, no Wi-Fi in the sleeping rooms, lots of cross ventilation.

## Building

Ground is broken on a new moon; the first stone is laid with an intention written inside it; the house opens on a full moon with a ceremony of gratitude (see [Aligned Dates](../docs/9-products-spaces.md#aligned-dates)).

## The Senses

Warm 2700 K light in the evening; the sound of the fountain and the sea; copal or palo santo at the welcome; local, seasonal plant-rich food; linen and wool to touch (see [Touch, Scent & Taste](../docs/9b-senses.md)).

## Programmes

Retreats of 3, 6 or 9 days, groups of 9 or 18, sessions of 54 or 108 minutes, rhythms that follow the local wet and dry seasons (see [Seasons around the World](../docs/3-elements-seasons.md#seasons-around-the-world)).

## Standards Win

Building codes, structural and fire safety, accessibility (ramps, door widths, escape routes) and food safety rules are kept as they are.
