# Applying QEA to an Event Flyer

∞ ∞ ∞

A worked example: a flyer for a weekly sunrise meditation by the sea, shared on social media and printed.

## Intention

> *"People feel invited, calm and welcome before they even arrive."*

The leading element is Water (the sea, calm), with a touch of Fire (the sunrise).

## Date and Time

The series begins on a new moon. Sessions start at 6:00 and last 54 minutes; the first gathering is announced 9 days ahead (see [Aligned Dates](../docs/8-time-rhythm.md#aligned-dates)).

## Size and Layout

- **Social media:** 1080 × 1350 px (4 : 5), a standard that stays as it is; a story at 1080 × 1920.
- **Print:** A5, a standard size.
- **Margins and spacing** from the aligned scale: 36, 54 and 72 px.
- **Three parts:** what it is, when and where, how to join.

## Colours

From the winter or summer palette, depending on the season where the event is: Deep Water for text, Coral as the sunrise accent, Frost or Warm Light as the background. Contrast checked.

## Words

Short and warm: *"Welcome the day by the sea."* No pressure, no countdown. A voluntary contribution named simply. The main line framed by ✦: `✦ Join Us at Sunrise ✦`.

## Symbols

A sun rising over three waves; ∞ ∞ ∞ as the divider; a Lotus Flower of Life at 9 % opacity in the background; quietly, a Grabovoi sequence that fits the intention in the footer.

## Print

Uncoated, recycled paper; the symbol embossed rather than printed (see [Touch, Scent & Taste](../docs/6-touch-scent-taste.md) and [Care for the Earth](../docs/care-for-the-earth.md)).
